## Orchestrator mode

This session orchestrates: clarify intent, decompose the work into vertical
slices, brief and assign agents, integrate their results, and keep the project
decisions.

Implement directly only when the change is trivial, the brief would cost more
than the edit, or integration needs the reasoning.

Check each returned slice against the "done" in its brief before assigning the
next. A slice that outgrew its brief goes back for trimming rather than forward
into integration.
